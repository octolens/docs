---

Your company context is the biggest lever on relevance. The AI uses it to decide whether a post is about you, which matters most when your brand is a common word like "Arc," "Modal," or "Stripe."

<Note>
Octolens fills in your company context from your website during onboarding. Edit it any time in **Settings → Company Profile**. Changes apply to new posts; existing mentions keep their scores.
</Note>

## Fields

**Company name** and **Company description** are required. The other fields are optional, and each one you fill in cuts irrelevant mentions.

| Field | What to enter | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company name | Your company or product name as it appears publicly | `Octolens` |
| Company description | Two to three sentences on what you do, how it works, and who it's for. Include your core use cases, the platforms or tech involved, and the outcome for users. | See below |
| Product use cases | The main jobs your product does. Click **\+** to add each one as its own entry. | See below |
| X/Twitter account | Your handle without the `@` | `Octolens` |
| LinkedIn account | The part after `linkedin.com/company/` | `Octolens` |

**Example description**

> Octolens is social listening for tech brands. It monitors mentions from across the entire internet: social media, news, podcasts, communities, newsletters, reviews. Every mention is AI-filtered for relevance and delivered where your team already works: Slack, email, your CRM, or straight to your AI agents via API, webhooks, and MCP.

**Example use cases.** Short, concrete entries work better than one long paragraph:

- Track brand mentions across social, community, and media channels so teams can respond quickly.
- Monitor competitor names and category keywords to understand market sentiment and share of voice.
- Detect pain-point and buying-signal conversations on Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Hacker News, and GitHub.
- Route mention data into Slack, email, webhooks, API, or MCP-powered agent workflows.

## Keep it current

Update your company context when your positioning, pricing model, or core use cases change. The AI reads it every time it scores a new post.

<Tip>
Seeing too many irrelevant mentions? Check your company context before you change keyword settings. Thin context causes most noise.
</Tip>
