---
title: Tracked Platforms
description: Overview of the platforms and data sources Octolens monitors for your keywords
---

Octolens monitors mentions everywhere people talk. Here's every platform we track, what we monitor, and how often.

<Warning>
When you first sign up to Octolens and choose keywords during onboarding, we go as far back as 7 days and find up to 100 posts per platform.

For any new keywords you add after onboarding, we also go as far back as 7 days and find up to 10 posts per platform.
</Warning>

| Data Source | What we monitor | Search intervals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **X/Twitter** | Keyword mentioned in tweet or comment | Depends on plan (Pro: hourly, Scale: under 5 minutes). No retweets. English tweets only. |
| **Reddit** | Keyword mentioned in post title, post body, and comments | Real time |
| **Hacker News** | Keyword mentioned in post and comments | Depends on plan (Pro: hourly, Scale: under 5 minutes) |
| **LinkedIn** | Keyword mentioned in public posts + your own brand-tagged mentions (via OAuth, set up in the platform page) | Depends on plan (Pro: hourly, Scale: under 5 minutes) |
| **Instagram** | Keyword mentioned in post or Reel captions from public accounts. Own-brand keywords only. | Once per day, 1 to 2 days after posting |
| **YouTube** | Keyword mentioned in video title or description | Depends on plan (Pro: hourly, Scale: under 5 minutes) |
| **Bluesky** | Keyword mentioned in post or comment | Real time |
| **TikTok** | Keyword mentioned in video description | Depends on plan (Pro: hourly, Scale: under 5 minutes) |
| **GitHub** | Keyword mentioned in GitHub issues | Depends on plan (Pro: hourly, Scale: under 5 minutes) |
| **Stack Overflow** | Keyword mentioned in post title and body | Runs once per day |
| **DEV** | Keyword mentioned in heading and description | Depends on plan (Pro: hourly, Scale: under 5 minutes) |
| [**Customer reviews**](/learn/platforms/customer-reviews) | Every review on a Trustpilot, App Store, or Google Play page you connect to a keyword. No keyword matching: connect the page and all its reviews count, with star rating and sentiment. | Once per day |
| **Podcasts (Scale only)** | Brand mentioned in transcript | Real time |
| **News (Scale only)** | Keyword mentioned in news publications (+150,000 sources) | Runs every 6 hours |
| **[Web](/learn/platforms/web) (Scale only)** | Keyword mentioned on indexed pages across the open web. Real time once a page is indexed, English only by default, and pages must have a Domain Rating of 30 or higher unless changed in Global keyword settings. | Real time once indexed |
| **[Newsletters](/learn/platforms/newsletter-list) (Scale only)** | Keywords mentioned in subject and body of newsletter | Real time |

<Info>
We are working on adding more data sources. Write us in our in-app chat or at hi@octolens.com if you have requests.
</Info>
