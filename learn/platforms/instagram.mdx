---
title: "Instagram"
description: "Track Instagram posts and Reels that mention your brand."
---

Octolens checks Instagram once a day for posts and Reels from public accounts whose caption mentions your keyword.

- **Own-brand keywords only.** Instagram is on for your brand keywords. Competitor and industry keywords don't collect from it.
- **A 1 to 2 day delay.** Octolens finds posts through Google, which indexes Instagram a day or two late. A post shows up in your feed one or two days after it goes live.
- **Captions only.** A post counts when its caption contains your keyword. Octolens skips posts where the keyword only appears in a username, a comment or an ad.
- **English by default.** Instagram follows your workspace's language settings, like X.

See [Tracked Platforms](/learn/platforms/overview) for every source Octolens monitors.
