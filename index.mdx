Octolens is an AI social listening tool for B2B SaaS and developer brands. It monitors Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Hacker News, GitHub, and [15+ platforms](/learn/platforms/overview) for mentions of your brand, your competitors, and the topics you care about. AI scores every mention for relevance, sentiment, and intent, so you only see the ones that matter.

Mentions go wherever your team, or your agents, already work: Slack, email, webhooks, the API, or AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT. The API, webhooks, and AI assistant connections are included on every plan.

## How Octolens works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose and tune your keywords">
    Add your brand name, competitors, and industry terms. Octolens uses your [company context](/quickstart/company-context) to score each mention, which is what lets you track common-word brands without drowning in noise. Fine-tune what each keyword matches in [keyword settings](/quickstart/keyword-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize mentions into feeds">
    Feeds are saved filters over your mentions. Start from a pre-built feed like Brand Mentions, Competitor Mentions, or Buy Intent, or build your own by platform, tag, sentiment, and relevance. See [Feeds](/quickstart/feeds).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send them to your stack">
    Deliver any feed to [Slack](/quickstart/connect-slack), email, or a [webhook](/quickstart/webhooks), or sync it into [Attio](/integrations/attio). Ask about your mentions in [Claude or ChatGPT](/mcp/v2/setup-guides), or pull them into your own tools with the [API](/api/v2/overview) and [CLI](/cli/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What teams use Octolens for

- **Reputation monitoring:** see what people say about you across social, reviews, and news, correct misinformation, and watch [sentiment](/learn/sentiment) shift over time.
- **Competitor monitoring:** catch the complaints, comparison threads, and switching moments from your competitors' users. Add competitors as [keywords](/quickstart/keywords) and filter with the Competitor Mention tag.
- **Customer feedback:** find feature requests, bug reports, and praise nobody sent you, [tagged by AI](/learn/ai-relevance) and routed to the team that owns them.
- **AI visibility:** find the public conversations on Reddit, forums, and reviews that shape what AI assistants recommend, and show up in them.
- **AI agents:** give your own agents real-time social data through the [API](/api/v2/overview), [webhooks](/quickstart/webhooks), the [CLI](/cli/overview), or the [Octolens MCP server](/mcp/v2/overview).

Founders, marketing teams, developer relations teams, and agencies use Octolens, including teams at Vercel, PostHog, Supabase, Prisma, Railway, and Tally. Agencies can run monitoring for each client in its own workspace.

## What every mention includes

- **Relevance:** High, Medium, or Low, based on your company context. See [AI Relevance & Tags](/learn/ai-relevance).
- **Sentiment:** Positive, Neutral, or Negative. See [Sentiment Analysis](/learn/sentiment).
- **Tags:** labels like Own Brand Mention, Competitor Mention, Buy Intent, and Bug Report that you can filter feeds by.
- **Context:** the platform, author, and link to the original post, plus a one-line explanation of why it matched your keyword.

## Where mentions come from

Octolens tracks Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Hacker News, GitHub, YouTube, Bluesky, TikTok, DEV, and Stack Overflow on every plan. The Scale plan adds podcasts, news, newsletters, and the open web. You can also connect Trustpilot, App Store, and Google Play pages to collect [customer reviews](/learn/platforms/customer-reviews).

See [Tracked platforms](/learn/platforms/overview) for what is monitored on each platform and how often.
