## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add keywords">
    Octolens uses your [company context](/quickstart/company-context) to score each mention for relevance, sentiment, and intent. That's how common-word brands stay free of noise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize mentions into feeds">
    Start with Brand Mentions, Competitor Mentions, or Buy Intent, or [build your own](/quickstart/feeds).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send them to your stack">
    Route feeds to [Slack](/quickstart/connect-slack), email, [webhooks](/quickstart/webhooks), or [Attio](/integrations/attio), or query them with the [API](/api/v2/overview) and [CLI](/cli/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every plan covers Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Hacker News, GitHub, YouTube, Bluesky, TikTok, DEV, and Stack Overflow. Scale adds podcasts, news, newsletters, and the web. You can also track [reviews](/learn/platforms/customer-reviews) from Trustpilot, the App Store, and Google Play. See [all platforms](/learn/platforms/overview).
