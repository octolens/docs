title: "ChatGPT"
description: "Connect Octolens to ChatGPT and ask about your mentions in plain language."
---

You add Octolens as an app in ChatGPT and sign in with your Octolens account. There's no API key to paste.

<Tip>
You can also start this from the app: **Settings → MCP → Setup Octolens MCP** walks you through the same steps.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Developer Mode">
    In ChatGPT, go to **Settings → Apps** and turn on Developer Mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    Create a new app with **OAuth** authentication and this URL:

    ```text
    https://app.octolens.com/api/mcp/v2
    ```

    ChatGPT finds the sign-in settings on its own, so you don't need a client ID or secret.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off OpenID Connect">
    Expand **Advanced Settings** and uncheck **OIDC enabled**. If you leave it on, sign-in fails.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and use it">
    Sign in to Octolens when ChatGPT asks. In a chat, click **+** and select Octolens.
  </Step>
</Steps>
